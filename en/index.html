Grandma’s legendary chocolate chip cookies

By Grandma Hildegard · ★★★★★ 4.9 (2,381 ratings)

Before we get to the recipe, I need to back up a little. It was a foggy Tuesday in the autumn of 1987 when I first understood that a cookie is more than a biscuit. The wind whistled through the shutters, and somewhere a dog barked. The dog becomes important later.

My husband Günther always used to say: “Hildegard, you can’t bake every day." Günther was wrong. Günther was often wrong, but that is a different story, which I tell further down in three parts.

Ingredients makes 12

  • 125 g (½ cup) soft butter
  • 100 g (½ cup) brown sugar
  • 50 g (¼ cup) white sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200 g (1⅔ cups) plain flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 150 g dark chocolate, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Cream the butter with both sugars for 2–3 minutes. Briefly mix in the egg and vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, baking soda and salt and stir in just until a dough forms.
  4. Fold in the chopped chocolate. Sneak a piece. It’s tradition.
  5. Shape 12 balls and space them well apart on the tray – they spread.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes: the edges are golden, the middle still looks soft. That is exactly right.
  7. Let them cool on the tray for 10 minutes. Only then do they firm up.

And the dog? His name was Biscuit. Enjoy. – Grandma
